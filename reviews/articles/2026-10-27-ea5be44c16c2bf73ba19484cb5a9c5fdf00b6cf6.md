### C1 — The simultaneous three-train stall is invented

- **Article location and quote:** line 11 says PR #456 and PR #462 “stalled all three trains simultaneously.”
- **Primary evidence:** [PR #456](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/456) records an October 22 candidate-link boundary. [October 22 acceptance #454](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/454) merged at 02:19 UTC and [October 23 acceptance #460](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/460) merged at 05:36 UTC, while the diagnostic-display repair [#462](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/462) did not merge until 06:22 UTC and changed no article or queue data. Those primary records cannot support one simultaneous blocker across all three article trains.
- **Correction:** report #456 as an October 22 action-decision repair and #462 as an October 23/current-request display repair. Preserve their separate chronology and drop the three-train causal claim.
- **Disposition:** open
