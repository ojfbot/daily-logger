The source omits the explicit `commitCount` frontmatter that the corrected October 21 article carries, and the clean build reproduces the wrong fallback value exactly.

Evidence: [article source and summary](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/blob/89b826e7ef715d317e4cea5e26a90f57a72c61d9/_articles/2026-10-22.md#L1-L35), [deployed JSON](https://log.jim.software/api/articles/2026-10-22.json), [daily-logger commits](https://api.github.com/repos/ojfbot/daily-logger/commits?since=2026-10-21T08%3A30%3A00Z&until=2026-10-22T08%3A30%3A00Z&per_page=100), [classic-space-venue commits](https://api.github.com/repos/ojfbot/classic-space-venue/commits?since=2026-10-21T08%3A30%3A00Z&until=2026-10-22T08%3A30%3A00Z&per_page=100), [collector author-date filter](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/blob/89b826e7ef715d317e4cea5e26a90f57a72c61d9/src/collect-context.ts#L128-L155).
