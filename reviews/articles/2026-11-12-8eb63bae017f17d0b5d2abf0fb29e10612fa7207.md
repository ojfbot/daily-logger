## Confirmed errors

### C1 — F-0 ran on a local session dataset, not a physical store machine or FleetRunner NAS stack

- **Article location and quote:** lines 11 and 48 say the F-0 and FleetRunner threads share “the same NAS image, the same host-local key material, the same operator adapter pattern,” and call `sf-store-001` “a physical store machine running the production NAS image.”
- **Primary evidence:** BookcaseDiscovery [PR #98](https://github.com/ojfbot/BookcaseDiscovery/pull/98) and its pinned [report](https://github.com/ojfbot/BookcaseDiscovery/blob/24ff5d248815713a360ede4d07b2cc72b0cfcb4c/evals/F0-frames/report.md) identify `sf-store-001` as a training-source session containing 616 frames from 15 clips. The report says the run occurred “on this Mac,” with a local catalog, offline model helpers, and network fetches refused. It does not use the FleetRunner physical-trial adapter, NAS service image, or host-local FleetRunner credentials.
- **Correction:** describe `sf-store-001` as the video-session dataset and say the evaluation ran locally on a Mac. Keep the FleetRunner physical-trial work and F-0 evaluation as independent workstreams without claiming shared NAS execution infrastructure.
- **Disposition:** open

### C2 — The F-0 result is not a reproducibility comparison against a frozen reference run

- **Article location and quote:** line 48 says machine agreement means “output label distributions are compared against a frozen reference run” and that failure would indicate nondeterminism or environment drift.
- **Primary evidence:** the pinned [pre-registration](https://github.com/ojfbot/BookcaseDiscovery/blob/24ff5d248815713a360ede4d07b2cc72b0cfcb4c/evals/F0-frames/prereg.md) defines machine agreement as agreement among the stack's own stages/readers, never accuracy. The [report](https://github.com/ojfbot/BookcaseDiscovery/blob/24ff5d248815713a360ede4d07b2cc72b0cfcb4c/evals/F0-frames/report.md) compares aggregate rates for different inputs: 205 video frames versus 31 high-resolution `pilot-000` stills. It records large expected differences and hypotheses about resolution, motion blur, reader coverage, and recurrence; it is not an exact-output replay or nondeterminism test.
- **Correction:** say the run measures internal stage/reader agreement on the video-frame sample and compares aggregate rates with the existing still-image caches. Do not interpret differences as failure, nondeterminism, or environment drift.
- **Disposition:** open
