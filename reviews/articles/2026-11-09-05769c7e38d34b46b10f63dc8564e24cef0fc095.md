## Confirmed errors

### C1 — Backup coverage excludes files the article says are included

- **Article location and quote:** the summary and lines 11 and 17 say “every SQLite fact ledger and classified data file” and “All other data files” are backed up.
- **Primary evidence:** the pinned [backup documentation](https://github.com/ojfbot/BookcaseDiscovery/blob/c4659cb037633cdf501076d0a29361f277524aa4/docs/backup.md#L24-L33) excludes the credentials file, restore-drill scratch roots, SQLite `-wal`, `-shm`, and `-journal` files in specified cases. The same document classifies fact, derived, re-fetchable, and unclassified data rather than promising an exception-free copy of every file.
- **Correction:** say the backup covers the classified data-root inventory subject to its documented credential, scratch-root, and SQLite side-file exclusions; do not say every classified or all other data file is included.
- **Disposition:** open

[BookcaseDiscovery #89](https://github.com/ojfbot/BookcaseDiscovery/pull/89) records a real first backup and restore drill, including 104,611 source files, 101,253 restored files, identical replay and dataset-build results, and clean scratch removal.
- **Correction:** describe a passing drill as verified object reads plus ledger replay and byte-identical dataset reconstruction, and acknowledge the recorded real-data drill while retaining its stated scope and limits.
