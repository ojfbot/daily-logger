## Confirmed errors

### C1 — Completed independent reviews are described as unfinished model pipelines

- **Article location and quote:** Lines 23–25 and 45–51, plus the first suggested action: review drafts allegedly open “as soon as an article commit is visible,” await proposer/verifier passes and receipts, and may be running, queued or stalled before promotion.
- **Primary evidence:** [PR #449](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/449) and [PR #450](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/450) already contain completed source-pinned records with seven and eight open confirmed findings respectively, validations, and durable agent handoffs. The same is true of [PR #421](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/421) and [PR #433](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/pull/433). Their next recorded step is supervised finding disposition and a decision whether to request a correction; none exposes an outstanding proposer/verifier pass, receipt-delivery queue, readiness promotion or label transition. The timings also contradict “as soon”: #449 opened about 14 hours after the October 24 merge, and #450 about 95 minutes after the October 25 merge.
- **Correction:** Describe these as completed proposed review records left draft for supervised disposition. Report their actual finding counts and next step; remove the model-pass, receipt-queue, promotion and pipeline-stall narrative.
- **Disposition:** `open`.

### C2 — The PR description is correspondence, not the canonical hash-bound review artifact

- **Article location and quote:** Lines 29–31 and draft context line 78: source commit and blob in the PR description are “the canonical artifact of record” and bind a proposer to a verifier.
- **Primary evidence:** The pinned [review discovery service](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/blob/acbfc2d7c204d9a706f9143f063cb6413cfb8c6c/api/_lib/article-review-service.ts#L62-L101) validates `reviews/registered/*.json`, loads the article at the registered source commit, checks the article blob and full-record blob, restricts the PR to the three review-data path classes, and treats the PR body separately as correspondence. The [schema](https://github.com/ojfbot/daily-logger/blob/acbfc2d7c204d9a706f9143f063cb6413cfb8c6c/shared/article-review.ts#L20-L37) puts the source commit, article blob, full-record path and full-record blob in the registered JSON. It defines no proposer/verifier identity or pass.
- **Correction:** Identify the registered JSON plus verified full-record Markdown and source article blob as the bound review record. The PR description may summarize those pins, but it is not the canonical record or a two-model binding mechanism.
- **Disposition:** `open`.
