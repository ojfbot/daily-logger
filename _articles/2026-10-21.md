## The decisions

### Classic Space Venue reads only fleet-runner's orchestration layer — cockpit and daily-logger are subjects, not feeds — *assistant-centric*

The operator ruled on 2026-10-20 (fourth round) that the Classic Space Venue binds to fleet-runner's read-projection output exclusively; morning-cockpit and daily-logger are entities that fleet-runner tracks, not data sources the venue queries directly. The mechanical consequence is a single-seam data dependency: the venue never needs to know the internal shape of any individual repo, only what fleet-runner has projected. This is the same constraint that makes the venue's arrival gate legible — if the venue ever needed to query cockpit or daily-logger directly, the arrival definition would have to expand to cover those interfaces too.

Tags: `wayfinder`, `assistant-centric`

### World-building pipeline before interface and agent binding — Blender as world hub — *model-behavior-as-design*

The fifth-round operator ruling puts the world-building pipeline first: a still image, then a Blender world, then a renderable browser or app deliverable.
