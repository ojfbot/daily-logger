## The decisions

### Independent review drafts open immediately after publication, not before — *model-behavior-as-design*

The editorial pipeline opens source-pinned review draft PRs (#449, #450) as soon as an article commit is visible, before any human reads the article. This decouples the review gate from the publish event — the article is already public while its independent review is still pending. That is a deliberate tradeoff: the system optimizes for publish latency over review-before-publish integrity. Whether that tradeoff is acceptable depends on the trust level of the automated publication step, which is currently fully bot-driven with no pre-publish human gate.

### Article blob and source commit hash are co-pinned at review-draft creation time — *security-as-emergent-ux*

Both draft review PRs record the exact article source commit SHA and the article blob hash at the moment the draft opens — in the PR description, as the canonical artifact of record. This is the mechanism that prevents a review from silently drifting to a newer article version: the verifier is bound to the same byte sequence the proposer evaluated. The blob hash is not redundant with the commit SHA; the commit can contain multiple files, and the blob hash isolates the specific article file. Without this isolation, a minor correction commit between proposer pass and verifier pass could produce a review that silently covers different content than was submitted. This property belongs in a formal ADR; currently it lives only in pipeline behavior and these daily notes. Reviewers who see the duplicated hash and consider removing it should know it is the tamper-evidence mechanism, not an accident.

### Five-day acceptance campaign on October 21 is complete; action backlog is not settled — *assistant-centric*

The pipeline is designed to preserve that distinction: article acceptance and action admission are separate authority gates, exercised independently. This is the same pattern as change approval and change execution in mature ITSM: collapsing them would mean every published article automatically authorized its own suggested work items. The gate exists to prevent that.
