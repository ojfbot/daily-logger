### D-series discipline: adopt-stack passes, not incremental syncs — *model-behavior-as-design*

The watch issue's rules of engagement (D43) are explicit: never sync or vendor on a delta alone — a material upstream change triggers a new /adopt-stack pass continuing the D-series. This prevents the common failure mode where an incremental dependency sync absorbs opinions embedded in new skill definitions without a deliberate evaluation pass.

For readers coming in cold: the D-series is the fleet's numbered decision log for adopt-stack evaluations. D43 is the 43rd entry. Each entry records what was evaluated, what was adopted, and — critically — what was evaluated and deliberately not adopted. 'We evaluated this and chose not to adopt' is a first-class outcome with its own D-series entry, not a non-event. The D-series lives in core and is referenced by issue and PR number. D1 through D42 are prior adopt-stack passes; D43 is the pass that created the Pocock watch issue.

The /adopt-stack command initiates a structured evaluation pass against the fleet's skill catalog. It is not an incremental sync — it is a full deliberate pass that either produces adoption commits or a D-series entry recording the rejection with rationale.

### Correspondence-speech-act ADR as fleet-level coordination primitive — *assistant-centric*

Core PR #495 (draft ADR: correspondence-speech-act-tiers) is the single in-flight structural decision crossing the weekend. The draft generalises the play-well cluster's CORR-* memo protocol into a fleet-wide coordination pattern: correspondence items are typed by speech act (assertion, request, commitment, declaration), each type carries different response obligations, and the register on GitHub mints identity.

In mechanical terms: a CORR-* memo typed as a 'commitment' obligates the receiving agent to track and report on its fulfillment; a memo typed as a 'request' obligates a response but not fulfillment; a 'declaration' changes state without requiring acknowledgment. Without this typing, all correspondence is treated as undifferentiated and response obligations are implicit — which means they are routinely missed or inconsistently applied across the fleet.

The lego-village-pipeline tracking issue #30 records its dependency on this ADR accepting. That dependency is real, not a tracking convenience: lego-village-pipeline's R2 design-package preflight (#22) cannot define its correspondence obligations until the fleet-wide typing is settled. What's holding #495 in draft after four days is not documented in this article — the draft's open questions are not enumerated here. That is an honest gap: the article cannot name what it doesn't know. The /pr-review action below is the correct next step to surface those open questions explicitly.
