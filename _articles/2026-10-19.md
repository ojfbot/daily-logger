The prompt-size concern in issue #386 remains real: the action-review pipeline assembles source material — held suggestions, identity records, rejection history — into complete prompts for two agents (a proposer and a verifier). Runtime evidence limits are 131,072 bytes (128 KiB) per source and 393,216 bytes (384 KiB) in total; source rejection stops before either action-agent call. Each complete prompt has its own 655,360-byte (640 KiB) guard before its corresponding call, so verifier-prompt overflow could occur after a proposer invocation. ## The decisions

### Combining duplicate workflow `env` blocks rather than adding a third — *tooling-for-iteration*

The YAML repair deleted the redundant `env:` header and its blank line rather than patching around the duplicate or restructuring the file. Strict js-yaml parsing rejects files with two top-level keys of the same name at the first duplicate occurrence — adding `ACTION_REVIEW_DISABLED` to the existing mapping is the structurally correct fix and leaves no surface for future writers to misread the file as intentionally split.

Tags: `editorial-pipeline`, `provenance`, `assistant-centric`

### Capping new action identities at two per article lifecycle with evidence requirements — *model-behavior-as-design*

The verified action registry introduced in #381 caps new suggestion identities at two per article lifecycle and requires factual evidence, goal evidence, and an independent verifier for each. Without this cap the registry grows unboundedly with every article pass, making duplicate detection unreliable and prompt-size management harder to bound. The tradeoff is real but bounded: legitimate suggestions beyond the cap are held, not dropped. The 138 historical suggestions are preserved as unadmitted records serving duplicate detection and rejection memory; they predate the current admission policy and have not passed its checks. That is Monday follow-through, not Sunday-shipped behavior; the historical two-new-identities lifecycle ceiling describes Sunday's source version.
