## The decisions

### Remove CI skip markers from automated PR commits — *security-as-emergent-ux*

PR #329 removes the skip signal instead of claiming required checks override it. Editorial PRs must produce the required statuses on their actual head through the repaired event path. Anti-recursion is not permission to bypass those checks.

Tags: `ci-cd`, `hardening`, `daily-logger`

### Confine the existing editorial PAT to the validated final push — *security-as-emergent-ux*

Trusted-base policy checks article-only direct-tree equality before the final push receives `GH_PAT`; checkout does not retain credentials. Workflow isolation and token permission scoping are distinct. The former was implemented; a newly narrowed GitHub App token was not.

Tags: `ci-cd`, `hardening`, `daily-logger`

### Refuse ambiguous policy repairs that could weaken existing rules — *security-as-emergent-ux*

Commit `8d90aad` rejects ambiguous repairs and preserves stronger existing rules rather than treating every existing policy as safe to replace. A refusal needs operator review of the run output; it is not evidence that the proposed repair was applied.

Tags: `ci-cd`, `security-scanning`, `hardening`, `core`

### Verify public rollout separately from private capability and rollback — *security-as-emergent-ux*

The public rollout reached 24 of 24 verified defaults, comprising eight already protected and 16 newly protected. The 27 private repositories remain a separate capability and policy decision. A single tooling commit makes its source change auditable, but Git history is not a rollback mechanism for server-side rulesets.
